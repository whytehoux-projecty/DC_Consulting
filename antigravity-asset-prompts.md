## Read this first (3 facts that change how the work must be split)

1. **Image models cannot make true SVG or ICO files, and they garble text.** So the work is split in two:
   - **Vector track (coding model writes the code):** logo, favicons, UI icons, patterns, avatar placeholder. SVG is code, so the coding model should author it by hand, not "generate" it as a picture. ICO and PNG sizes are exported from the SVG by a script.
   - **Raster track (image model):** the editorial illustrations only. All real words (OG card text, etc.) are added afterwards by the coding model using the site's real fonts, never by the image model.
2. **Nothing generated may pretend to be real.** This company's credibility is its product. Generated images must be clearly illustrative: no fake photos of "our mine, refinery or vault", no invented people as the team or CEO, no fake certificates, no logos or seals of SGS, GIA, Brinks, Lloyd's, the Kimberley Process or any real organisation. Real photography replaces the illustrations later (shot list at the end).
3. **The company name is provisional.** Do the logo (Prompt 2) last if you may still rename the company, or build it exactly as written, which makes the name easy to swap. Also run a trademark search on any final name and mark. The agent cannot do that legally for you.

## Reference: global quality standards (every asset must pass)

- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA. Text and meaningful icons at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and graphics), checked against the real background colour. No information conveyed by colour alone. Decorative images get `alt=""`. Meaningful images get honest, specific alt text.
- **SVG:** valid XML, `viewBox` set, no embedded raster images, no scripts, no external references, no fonts (text converted to outlines), optimised with SVGO, with `<title>` where it is meaningful. Icons use `currentColor`.
- **Raster images:** generated at least 2048 px on the long edge, then exported at needed sizes. sRGB colour. Delivered as AVIF and WebP with a JPEG fallback (PNG only where transparency is needed). Always `width` and `height` attributes (no layout shift). `loading="lazy"` except the first-screen image. Hero at most 120 KB, cards at most 60 KB each.
- **Consistency:** one illustration style across the whole set (same line weight, same palette, same lighting direction, same level of detail). If two assets look like they came from different artists, regenerate one.
- **Originality:** no stock-looking clichés (handshakes, glowing globes, generic gears, hard-hat cartoons, chrome 3D). No generic template look.
- **Licensing and ownership:** only assets the agent generated or wrote itself, and fonts under open licences (Source Serif 4 and Inter are SIL OFL). No scraping, no copying existing logos or artwork.
