# DC Consulting — Brand Identity Usage Guide

## Identity Rationale & Concept Choice
From three explored concept directions (Waypoint, Chain of Custody, and Precision Bastion), **Concept 3 (Custody Route Mark)** was selected:
1. **Distinctiveness**: The monogram unites the letters 'D' and 'C' into an unbroken route geometry from origin to destination, capturing physical execution rather than abstract financial speculation.
2. **Legibility at 16px**: High internal contrast and half-pixel stroke snapping prevent line merging at small favicon sizes.
3. **Geometric Precision**: Clean architectural radii paired with a single solid bronze custody notch (#C1A066) at the origin axis.
4. **Scalability**: Works flat without gradients, drop-shadows or fine lines.
5. **Wordmark Harmony**: Pairs with Source Serif 4 headings and Inter typography.

## Clear Space & Minimum Sizes
- **Clear Space**: A minimum exclusion zone equal to 1x the mark height must surround the logo on all sides.
- **Minimum Digital Size**: 24px height for horizontal lockup; 16px for standalone mark.
- **Minimum Print Size**: 12mm width for the standalone mark.

## Forbidden Uses
- Do NOT distort, rotate, stretch or slant the logo.
- Do NOT add drop shadows, outer glows, bevels or gradients.
- Do NOT replace the bronze accent with another colour.
- Do NOT place the navy logo on dark backgrounds (use `logo-reverse.svg`).

*Note: The company name and mark are provisional. A formal trademark clearance search should be conducted in the relevant jurisdictions prior to global commercial launch.*
